# Minutide identity

The primary mark is a flowing aqua M on midnight blue, joining the app's name
with a sense of tide and continuous time. Use the same artwork on the Home
Screen and the welcome page. The icon contains no text or baked-in outer mask.

- Generated master: `design/brand/minutide-logo-master.png` (1254 × 1254, opaque).
- App icon: `Stiint/Assets.xcassets/MinutideIcon.appiconset/MinutideIcon.png`
  (1024 × 1024, opaque RGB PNG).
- Welcome image: `Stiint/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.imageset/MinutideLogo.png`.
- App target's icon setting is `MinutideIcon`. The original Stiint icon source
  is retained for history and is no longer selected by the app target.

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. The original output is kept
on the SSD; macOS `sips` only resampled it to the required icon dimensions.
No external image service or credential was added to the app.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create the finished primary iOS app icon for Minutide, a calm, polished time tracking app whose name joins minute and tide. Square 1024 by 1024 pixels, full bleed opaque background, no rounded outer corners (iOS applies its own mask). An original, extremely simple and distinctive abstract letter M formed by two broad fluid tide-like curves, with a subtle sense of continuous motion and measured rhythm. One integrated symbol, legible at 40px, generous breathing room, mark occupies about 58 percent of the square. Deep midnight blue background. A luminous but restrained pale aqua to clear sky-blue mark with clean geometry and soft continuous curves; mostly flat, tiny tonal variation only, crisp edges. Sophisticated friendly independent software identity, tactile clarity without a 3D object, calm premium quality. No text, no wordmark, no letters beyond the abstract M, no stopwatch face, no clock hands, no checkmark, no charts, no tiny ticks, no separate decorative waves, no drop shadow, no border, no mockup, no grid or presentation board. Produce one production-ready app icon, not a sheet of options.
